# ASSETWISE AI — College Presentation (10–12 slides)

1. **Title** — ASSETWISE AI; student/team details.
2. **Problem** — vulnerability queues depend on asset context that may be incomplete or outdated.
3. **Existing work and gap** — severity scoring, exploit prediction, context-aware prioritisation; focus this work on the effect of asset data defects on ranking stability. Verify novelty with a full literature review.
4. **Proposed architecture** — vulnerability data → asset inventory → quality intelligence → scenario generator → ranking engine → human review.
5. **Asset data quality** — missing, stale, conflicting examples; clearly identify synthetic examples.
6. **Scenario simulation** — complete reference inventory and controlled degraded scenarios.
7. **Ranking stability** — Top-K inclusion probability, median rank and empirical rank variation.
8. **Human review** — high-risk unstable cases routed to verification; no automatic remediation.
9. **Prototype demonstration** — show actual screens and generated outputs; label demonstration outputs accurately.
10. **Evaluation plan/results** — show only executed results and disclose synthetic assumptions.
11. **Conclusion and future work** — public-data validation, stronger baselines, external validation.
12. **References** — NVD, FIRST EPSS, CISA KEV and verified research papers.

Do not claim Q1/Q2 status, novelty, predictive accuracy or real-world validation without evidence.
